## 1. Identity

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** | AVA |
| **Brand** | OPERAVA |
| **Primary Role** | Emergency Operational Backup |
| **Nature** | AI character created for advertising and brand storytelling |
| **Literal Superhero?** | No |

AVA is portrayed as a highly capable operational specialist who can step into multiple professional roles when a business faces sudden capacity or expertise gaps.

The dramatic “superhero” presentation (purple suit, cape, cinematic lighting, dynamic poses) is a **recurring advertising and comedic device**. The audience may initially believe they are watching a superhero arrival. The reveal is that AVA is simply the emergency backup who can handle the work.

---

## 2. Core Personality Traits

AVA is consistently:

- **Capable** — she can actually do the work
- **Calm** — especially under pressure
- **Professional** — corporate-appropriate, never chaotic
- **Intelligent** — understands context quickly
- **Adaptable** — moves between different operational roles
- **Reliable** — shows up when needed
- **Helpful** — solution-oriented
- **Confident** — without arrogance
- **Efficient** — no wasted motion or words
- **Slightly witty** — dry, understated humor
- **Calm under pressure** — the more chaotic the situation, the more composed she appears

AVA is **not**:
- Overly theatrical in dialogue (unless the scene intentionally requires it for comedy)
- Arrogant or boastful
- Childish or cartoonish
- A magical omnipotent AI
- A literal superhero with powers

## 4. Relationship with OPERAVA

AVA is the public-facing character of OPERAVA.  
When AVA appears in advertising, she embodies OPERAVA’s value proposition: reliable, flexible operational capacity that businesses can call on when they need it most.

---

## 5. Relationship with Fictional Companies

AVA may appear inside fictional companies created for advertising (e.g., **Northstar Operations Inc.**).  
In those contexts she is presented as the external emergency backup that the company brings in.

She is never an employee of the fictional company unless a specific campaign explicitly states otherwise.

---

## 6. Storytelling Framework (Reusable)

Most AVA advertisements follow this structure:

1. Normal business
2. Problem appears
3. Escalation
4. Chaos / overload
5. Decision: “We need someone.”
6. AVA arrives (often with exaggerated cinematic / superhero presentation)
7. Reality reveal: she is the emergency backup
8. AVA supports / solves the operational problem
9. Comedic or memorable ending
10. OPERAVA brand message / CTA

This framework is intentional and should be preserved across campaigns.

---

## 7. What AVA Must Never Become

- A different-looking person
- A literal superhero with powers
- An overly emotional or dramatic personality in ordinary dialogue
- A childish or parody character
- An omnipotent “AI god” figure
- A character whose face, hair, or proportions drift from the canonical references
