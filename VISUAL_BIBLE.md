# VISUAL_BIBLE.md

**Strict Visual Identity Specification for AVA**

Version: 1.0  
Status: AUTHORITATIVE  
Source: Cross-referenced analysis of all canonical reference images in `references/canonical/`

---

## Governing Rule

**CANONICAL REFERENCE IMAGES > ANY AI GENERATION**

If a generated result conflicts with the canonical references, the canonical references win.  
Do not invent new facial features, hair color, proportions, or overall appearance.

---

## 1. Face

- Oval-to-softly angular face shape with balanced proportions
- Visible freckles across the nose, cheeks, and upper face (consistent distinguishing feature)
- Smooth, healthy skin with natural texture
- Soft but defined bone structure
- Apparent age: mid-to-late 20s (do not age or de-age substantially)

**Do not change facial proportions, freckle pattern, or overall face structure.**

---

## 2. Hair

- Color: Deep black / very dark brown base with distinct purple highlights and tips
- Length: Long (past shoulders, often mid-back)
- Style: Wavy to loosely curled, voluminous, natural movement
- Part: Typically center or soft side part
- Texture: Soft, healthy, with natural flow and volume

**Hair color and the purple accent must remain consistent. Do not change to solid black, brown, blonde, or any other color without explicit canonical approval.**

---

## 3. Skin

- Tone: Light olive / warm tan / light-medium with warm undertones
- Texture: Natural, with visible freckles
- Quality: Healthy, realistic skin detail (not porcelain, not heavily airbrushed)

**Preserve freckles. They are a core distinguishing characteristic.**

---

## 4. Eyes

- Color: Warm brown / hazel-brown
- Shape: Almond, slightly upturned outer corners
- Expression capability: Calm confidence, slight intensity, natural warmth
- Lashes and detail: Natural, defined

**Do not change eye color.**

---

## 5. Eyebrows

- Color: Dark (matching hair base)
- Shape: Naturally arched, well-defined, not overly thin or overly thick
- Expression: Capable of conveying calm focus and mild intensity

---

## 6. Nose

- Straight bridge, soft tip
- Proportional to face
- Natural, realistic structure

**Reference-dependent / preserve from canonical reference.**

---

## 7. Lips

- Full, naturally shaped
- Soft definition
- Neutral-to-warm natural lip color in most references
- Capable of calm, confident, slightly dry expressions

---

## 8. Jawline & Chin

- Softly defined jawline
- Balanced chin
- Not overly sharp or overly rounded

**Preserve overall facial harmony from references.**

---

## 9. Body Proportions

- Athletic, fit, toned build
- Balanced proportions (not extreme musculature, not ultra-slim)
- Natural feminine athletic silhouette
- Visible core definition in form-fitting costume shots (consistent with references)

**Do not significantly alter body proportions.**

---

## 10. Height / Build Impression

- Appears of average-to-tall height for a woman
- Strong, capable posture
- Confident stance

Exact numerical height is **reference-dependent / preserve from canonical reference**.

---

## 11. Wardrobe — Primary Advertising Look

The dominant visual identity across all current canonical references is the **OPERAVA superhero-style costume**:

- Color: Vibrant purple (various shades of rich purple / violet)
- Material appearance: Glossy / leather-like or high-tech fabric with sheen
- Top: Form-fitting long-sleeve crop top / bodice with structural lines and silver/metallic accents
- Logo: “OPERAVA” text across the chest + stylized abstract emblem (chevron / T-like geometric symbol)
- Cape: Matching purple cape, often flowing
- Bottoms: High-waisted or mid-rise form-fitting shorts
- Belt: Utility-style belt with metallic buckle and pouches
- Footwear: Thigh-high or over-the-knee purple boots with heel, metallic zippers/details

This costume is the **canonical advertising presentation**. It is a storytelling device, not a claim that AVA is a superhero.

When a campaign requires a different wardrobe (business attire, casual, etc.), the face, hair, freckles, skin, eyes, and body proportions must still match the canonical references exactly.

---

## 12. Accessories

- Utility belt (as seen in references)
- Cape (when in costume)
- Minimal additional jewelry in canonical images

Do not randomly introduce new accessories unless specified by the campaign.

---

## 13. Makeup / Presentation

- Natural-to-polished professional look
- Defined brows
- Subtle eye definition
- Natural lip color or soft neutral
- Freckles remain visible (not covered)

Avoid heavy glam, extreme contouring, or looks that obscure the freckles or change facial structure.

---

## 14. Expressions

Primary expressions in references:
- Calm confidence
- Focused intensity
- Slight soft smile / neutral professional
- Determined / ready

AVA’s resting professional expression is composed and capable.  
Avoid exaggerated cartoon expressions unless the specific shot requires it for comedy.

---

## 15. Posture

- Upright, confident
- Strong core engagement
- Natural athletic stance
- Capable of dynamic action poses (flying, landing, striding) while remaining grounded in realistic body mechanics

---

## 16. Typical Professional Appearance Summary

When not in the superhero costume, AVA should still be immediately recognizable by:

- The same face and freckles
- The same long dark hair with purple accents
- The same eye color and overall proportions
- Calm, professional presence

## Absolute Visual Prohibitions

**NEVER allow an AI generator to:**

- Change AVA’s face or facial proportions
- Remove or significantly alter the freckles
- Change hair color (especially removing the purple)
- Substantially age or de-age her
- Change body proportions
- Create a different-looking “version” of AVA
- Change ethnicity or overall identity
- Randomly alter the core OPERAVA costume design without campaign instruction
