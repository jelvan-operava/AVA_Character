# CONTINUITY.md

**Continuity Rules for AVA Across All Media**

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

---

## NEVER CHANGE (Identity Lock)

Unless an explicit canonical update has been approved and documented in `VERSIONING.md`, **NEVER CHANGE**:

- AVA’s identity
- AVA’s face
- Facial proportions
- Freckle pattern
- Hair (style base, length, color, purple accents)
- Hair color
- Apparent age
- Body proportions
- Distinguishing visual features
- Overall physical appearance
- Ethnicity / overall identity presentation

These are locked.

---

## NEVER RANDOMIZE (When Continuity Is Required)

When producing sequential clips or maintaining scene continuity, **do not randomly change**:

- Background characters (faces, clothing, positions)
- Office architecture and layout
- Furniture placement and design
- Lighting direction, color temperature, intensity
- Weather or time of day (unless the story requires a time jump)
- Company branding and signage
- Props and their positions
- Camera geography and orientation relative to the space
- Clothing of recurring characters
- Character placement in the frame at the start of a new clip

The next clip must feel like a continuation, not a restart.

---

## Multi-Clip Continuity Principle

```
CLIP N final frame / state
        ↓
CLIP N+1 must inherit:
  - Character identity
  - Character location
  - Character pose / approximate posture
  - Character expression / emotional state
  - Camera position and direction
  - Lighting
  - Environment
  - Props
  - Wardrobe
  - Background people
  - Story state
```

Do not reset the environment between clips.

---

## Dialogue & Performance Continuity

- Emotional state carries forward
- Conversational energy carries forward
- Lip synchronization must match the written dialogue
- No sudden unexplained changes in demeanor unless scripted

---

## Brand & Company Continuity

Within a campaign (especially Northstar):

- Company name, logo treatment, and visual language stay consistent
- Office design language stays consistent
- Employee wardrobe style stays consistent

---

## Exception Process

Any intentional break in continuity (time jump, location change, costume change) must be:

1. Explicitly written into the campaign or video document
2. Reflected in the prompt as a deliberate transition
3. Not left to the AI to invent
