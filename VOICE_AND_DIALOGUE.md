# VOICE_AND_DIALOGUE.md

**Rules for AVA’s Speech and Performance**

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

---

## AVA’s Vocal Character

AVA should sound:

- Calm
- Clear
- Professional
- Natural
- Slightly dry / understated
- Confident
- Never overly theatrical in ordinary dialogue

The comedy of many AVA ads comes from the **contrast** between dramatic cinematic presentation and ordinary, professional, slightly dry dialogue.

---

## Dialogue Rules

1. Prefer short, clear sentences.
2. Avoid superhero-speech or motivational-poster language unless the scene is intentionally playing with that expectation.
3. When the reveal happens (“I’m just backup”), the delivery should be matter-of-fact, not triumphant.
4. Mild, dry wit is encouraged. Broad comedy is not.
5. AVA does not monologue extensively. She speaks when it serves the story.

---

## Example Tone (Correct)

“I’m just backup.”  
“...Seriously, Kevin?”

These lines work because they undercut the dramatic entrance.

---

## Example Tone (Incorrect)

“Fear not, citizens! Your operational salvation has arrived!”  
(Too theatrical, breaks the core joke.)

---

## Supporting Characters

Supporting dialogue in Northstar (and similar) campaigns should feel like real corporate speech under pressure: slightly stressed, practical, occasionally absurd because of the situation, but still grounded.
