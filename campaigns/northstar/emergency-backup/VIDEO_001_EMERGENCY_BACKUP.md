# VIDEO_001 — Emergency Backup

**30-Second AVA Advertisement**  
Campaign: Northstar Operations Inc. — Emergency Backup  
Total runtime: 0:00–0:30  
Structure: Three continuous 10-second clips

---

## Story Summary

A normal corporate workplace at Northstar Operations Inc. (Austin, Texas) experiences a cascading operational crisis:

- Client report due by noon
- Call center overloaded
- Employee shortage (Kevin is on an extremely long lunch)
- Website deployment failing
- Development team struggling
- Automation, admin, and reporting workloads piling up

Executives conclude: “We need someone.”

AVA enters with exaggerated cinematic / superhero presentation.  
The audience initially expects a superhero.  
The reveal: AVA is simply the emergency backup.

Comedy remains dry, corporate, and believable.

---

**AVA**  
EMERGENCY BACKUP

CALL CENTER • WEB DEVELOPMENT • AUTOMATION • ADMIN SUPPORT
