# Northstar Operations Inc. — Campaign Bible

**Fictional advertising environment for AVA**

Status: AUTHORITATIVE for all Northstar campaigns

---

## Company

**Name:** Northstar Operations Inc.  
**Location:** Austin, Texas, USA  
**Type:** Modern mid-to-large U.S. corporate company  
**Nature:** Fictional — exists purely for advertising and storytelling purposes

Northstar should feel completely believable as a real American company.

---

## Industry & Business

General operations / services company with:

- Client-facing work and reporting obligations
- An internal call center / customer support function
- A software / web development team
- Administrative and reporting workloads
- Automation and operational processes

Exact industry vertical can flex per campaign as long as the environment remains consistent.

---

## Office Environment

- Contemporary open-plan office with glass meeting rooms
- Call-center area with headsets and multiple monitors
- Development / engineering zone with dual monitors and code on screens
- Executive / management meeting rooms
- Operational status dashboards on large displays
- Neutral modern corporate color palette (grays, whites, blues, with occasional accent)
- Realistic fluorescent / LED office lighting mixed with natural light from windows

---

## Corporate Culture

- Professional but under pressure
- Employees are competent but overloaded
- Mild workplace absurdity emerges under stress (the Kevin lunch situation, etc.)
- Management is practical and decisive when forced to act

---

## Visual Identity (Northstar)

- Clean, modern, slightly generic corporate branding
- Avoid overly distinctive logos that would conflict with OPERAVA’s own brand presence
- Consistent office architecture across all Northstar videos

---

## Typical Employees

- Call-center agents and supervisors
- Software developers / engineers
- Administrative staff
- Operations managers
- Executives (CEO, COO)

Clothing: contemporary business-casual to professional office attire. Consistent across clips.

---

## Technology Environment

- Modern laptops and dual-monitor setups
- Large wall-mounted operational dashboards
- Standard enterprise software look (nothing that dates the production)

---

## Key Recurring Locations Within Northstar

1. Open office / operations floor
2. Call-center cluster
3. Development team area
4. Executive meeting room
5. Operational status dashboard wall

These locations must remain consistent across the Emergency Backup campaign and any future Northstar stories.

---

## Continuity Rule for Northstar

All Northstar videos share the same company, the same office design language, and the same underlying reality.  
Do not reinvent the company for each new ad.
