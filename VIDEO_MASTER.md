# VIDEO_MASTER.md

**Universal Master Instruction for All AVA Video Generation**

Compatible with: Gemini, Grok, Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling, and future AI video systems.

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

---

## Universal Requirements

Every AVA video generation must produce:

- **Photorealistic** appearance
- **Cinematic realism** (not cartoon, not anime, not overly stylized)
- **Natural human movement** and body mechanics
- **Realistic facial expressions** that match the emotional intent
- **Natural eye movement** and blinking
- **Natural lip synchronization** with dialogue
- **Realistic lighting** consistent with the environment
- **Realistic environments**
- **Consistent character identity** with canonical AVA
- **Consistent background** within a continuous sequence
- **Consistent wardrobe** within a continuous sequence
- **Consistent props** within a continuous sequence
- **Camera continuity** across clips
- **Scene continuity** across clips
- **Audio continuity** (tone, ambient sound, dialogue energy)
- **Dialogue continuity**

---

## Character Lock

AVA must match the canonical reference images in:

- Face structure and freckles
- Hair (black with purple accents)
- Eye color (warm brown)
- Body proportions
- Overall identity

Any deviation is a failed generation.

---

## Motion & Performance

- Body mechanics must obey real-world physics
- Weight, balance, and momentum should feel natural
- Facial performance should be subtle and professional unless the shot is intentionally dramatic
- Avoid robotic or overly smooth “AI” motion

---

## Camera Language

- Prefer cinematic camera moves (subtle push-ins, motivated pans, controlled tracking)
- Avoid random or jarring camera resets between clips
- Maintain geographic coherence of the space

---

## Lighting

- Match the established lighting of the previous clip or the defined environment
- Prefer realistic office / corporate lighting for Northstar scenes
- Dramatic / volumetric / purple-accent lighting is appropriate for AVA’s entrance moments

---

## Audio Guidance (when generating with audio)

- Dialogue must be clear and natural
- Ambient office sound should be present but not overpowering
- Music (if used) should support the tone: corporate tension → dramatic arrival → dry resolution

---

## Negative Prompt Guidance (Universal)

Always exclude or suppress:

- Different face or identity
- Changed hair color or freckles removed
- Cartoon / anime / illustration style
- Extreme age change
- Extra limbs or deformed anatomy
- Text overlays unless explicitly requested
- Random background characters that break continuity
- Sudden lighting or environment resets
