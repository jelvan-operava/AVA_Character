# STORYTELLING_FRAMEWORK.md

**Reusable Narrative Structure for AVA Advertisements**

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

---

Most AVA advertisements should follow this arc:

1. **Normal Business**  
   Establish a believable workplace.

2. **Problem**  
   A concrete operational issue appears.

3. **Escalation**  
   The problem grows or multiplies.

4. **Chaos / Overload**  
   The company is clearly overwhelmed.

5. **Decision**  
   “We need someone.”

6. **AVA Arrives**  
   Often with exaggerated cinematic / superhero-style presentation.

7. **Reality Reveal**  
   AVA is the emergency operational backup — not a literal superhero.

8. **Support / Resolution**  
   AVA addresses the operational need (or is positioned to).

9. **Comedic or Memorable Ending**  
   Frequently a dry line that undercuts the drama (e.g., the Kevin joke).

10. **OPERAVA Brand Message**  
    Clear statement of AVA’s role and service areas.

---

This framework is intentional.  
It creates recognition across campaigns while allowing new stories and settings.
