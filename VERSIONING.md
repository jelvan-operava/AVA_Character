# VERSIONING.md

**How AVA Identity Changes Are Handled**

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

---

## Principle

Do not silently alter canonical identity.

Any intentional change to AVA’s appearance, personality definition, or core role must be deliberate, documented, and versioned.

---

## Process for Identity Updates

1. **Document the proposed change**  
   What is changing and why.

2. **Update canonical references**  
   Add new approved images to `references/canonical/` (do not delete historical ones without reason).

3. **Update VISUAL_BIBLE.md and/or CHARACTER_BIBLE.md**  
   Reflect the new canonical state.

4. **Update affected campaigns**  
   Note any impact on existing campaign documents.

5. **Preserve history**  
   Keep previous canonical images and documents accessible (via git history or an archive folder).

6. **Record the version**  
   Add an entry below.
